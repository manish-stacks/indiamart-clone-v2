# IndiaMART-style B2B + B2C Marketplace: Requirements Traceability Matrix

## Status Definitions
- **Planned**: Architected, schemas defined, pending vertical slice implementation.
- **In Progress**: Currently being coded or integrated across layers.
- **Implemented**: Frontend, backend, database, and permissions complete.
- **Tested**: Verified with unit/integration/type checks.
- **Blocked**: Waiting on external dependency or prerequisite.

---

| ID | Feature Domain | Sub-Feature / Capability | Frontend Scope | Backend & API Scope | Database Scope | Security & Authorization | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **REQ-01** | **Identity & Auth** | Registration, Login, JWT tokens (Access + Refresh) | Next.js Login/Register forms, Session context, Token storage | NestJS AuthModule, JWT strategy, bcrypt hash, Refresh token rotation | `users`, `sessions`, `refresh_tokens`, `roles` | Rate limiting, brute-force protection, bcrypt salt 10, HttpOnly cookie | **Implemented & Tested (Phase 1)** |
| **REQ-02** | **RBAC & Business Membership** | User roles (Buyer, Seller, Admin, Support) + Business membership (Owner, Manager, Staff) | Dynamic navigation by role, role-based page protection | NestJS RolesGuard, BusinessMemberGuard, permission checks | `roles`, `permissions`, `user_roles`, `business_members` | Server-side authorization, business resource isolation | **Implemented & Tested (Phase 1)** |
| **REQ-03** | **Business Storefronts** | Business profiles, GSTIN, Indian addresses, verification status, contact cards | Business profile page, public storefront (`/store/[slug]`), edit profile form | BusinessModule (`/api/v1/businesses`), slug resolution, public profile DTO | `businesses`, `business_addresses`, `business_documents` | Private doc protection, only business owner/manager can edit | **Implemented & Tested (Phase 1)** |
| **REQ-04** | **Product Catalogue** | Products, nested categories, images, specifications, B2B MOQ, price tiers, B2C unit price | Catalogue browsing, `/categories`, `/products/[slug]`, product cards, filters | ProductModule (`/api/v1/products`), CategoryModule (`/api/v1/categories`) | `categories`, `products`, `product_images`, `product_variants`, `price_tiers` | Seller owns product, unapproved/draft visibility restrictions | **Implemented & Tested (Phase 1)** |
